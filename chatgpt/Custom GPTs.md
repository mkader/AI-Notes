Custom GPTs are task-specific AI assistants you can fine-tune for your needs: no coding, no headache. You decide how they act, what tools they use, and who gets access.

Here are 10 standout GPTs you can try for free:
1. Consensus: Delivers research-backed answers by searching academic studies (with citations)
2. Canva GPT: Design social posts, decks, and branding assets right inside ChatGPT
3. Video GPT by VEED: Edit videos with prompts—add subtitles, voiceovers, and more
4. Tutor Me: Practice problems + step-by-step help, like Khan Academy but conversational
5. Designer GPT: Plan and code websites with creative input and design feedback
6. Video Summarizer AI: Shrinks long-form YouTube videos into digestible takeaways
7. Books GPT: Offers personalized reading recs and deep literary convos
8. Mia AI: Your personal reflection coach for clarity and growth
9. The Negotiator: Sharpen your skills with AI-powered negotiation practice
10. Slide Maker GPT: Turn bullet points into full presentations—yes, really

How to go from a bad prompt to a good prompt. 

1. Go to ChatGPT.
2. On the left menu > click on “GPTs”.
3. Search for "Prompt Maker".
4. Type your bad prompt (= what you want).
5. The Prompt Maker will generate a better version.
