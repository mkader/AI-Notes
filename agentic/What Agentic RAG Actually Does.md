* What Agentic RAG Actually Does (And Why Your LLM Needs It)

* RAG is growing up. And it’s getting smarter, agent-level smart.

  1. Traditional RAG pulls data.
  1. Agent-driven RAG works the data.

* Here’s how it’s leveling up:
  1. Queries get split into sub-questions
  1. Specialized agents handle retrieval, validation, and reasoning
  1. Agents reflect, reroute, and refine in real time

* The system won’t stop until it has an answer that actually makes sense

  1. It’s not just “fetch and print” anymore.
  1. It’s “think, fetch, re-think, solve.”

* Use cases? 
  1. Think enterprise research, clinical diagnostics, or legal automation - anywhere “just getting data” isn’t enough.
