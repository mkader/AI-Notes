Here are the 10 free AI learning platforms from the companies actually building AI:

1. Anthropic: anthropic.skilljar.com - 13 free self-paced courses, covering Claude basics, prompt engineering, the Claude API, MCP and Claude Code. Free certificates included. Launched March 2026.

2. Google: grow.google/ai - AI learning hub, includes AI Essentials, Prompting Essentials and new AI Professional Certificate. Covers Gemini, NotebookLM, and real workplace AI tasks. No prior experience needed.

3. Meta: ai.meta.com/resources - open-source AI education hub, covers Llama models, PyTorch, deep learning fundamentals, and how to build and deploy AI apps using Meta's open-source stack.

4. NVIDIA: learn.nvidia.com - The most technical on the list, deep learning, GPU computing, generative AI, and RAG pipelines. Courses built by the team behind the hardware powering most AI in the world. Some premium courses periodically made free.

5. Microsoft: learn.microsoft.com/ai - Azure AI fundamentals, Microsoft Copilot training, and AI-900 certification prep. Includes interactive labs where you actually deploy and test AI services. Great for enterprise and business users.

6. OpenAI: academy.openai.com OpenAI's official academy, covers GPT models, the OpenAI API, prompt engineering, and building AI-powered products. Straight from the team behind ChatGPT.

7. IBM: skillsbuild.org IBM's free learning platform with courses on AI fundamentals, machine learning, data science, and AI ethics. Comes with free digital badges and certificates you can add to LinkedIn.

8. AWS: skillbuilder.aws Amazon's AI/ML training hub, covers generative AI, cloud AI services, and hands-on tools like Amazon Bedrock and SageMaker. Built for developers and business professionals alike.

9. DeepLearning AI: deeplearning.ai Founded by Andrew Ng, short courses (1–2 hrs each) co-built with OpenAI, LangChain, Anthropic, Google, and AWS. Covers prompt engineering, LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and more. The go-to for practical GenAI skills.

10. Hugging Face: huggingface.co/learn Hands-on courses from the team behind the world's biggest open-source AI model hub. Covers NLP, transformers, diffusion models, and AI agents, ideal for builders and developers.
