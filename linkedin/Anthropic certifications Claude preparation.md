### Anthropic certifications Claude preparation

#### https://www.linkedin.com/in/rania-khemiri/
* Completed all current Anthropic certifications. 
* If your goal is simply to collect badges, doing all four makes sense.
* If your goal is to better understand how to design and build agentic systems, I’d prioritize:
  1. Architect Foundations - contains several real-world scenarios, with multiple questions tied to each one, which makes the exam feel more engaging than a series of unrelated multiple-choice questions.
  2. Certified Developer
  3. Architect Professional - deeper into the architectural thinking behind building agentic systems.
* I’d skip the Associate exam unless you specifically want to complete the entire certification path.
* Preparation
  * no dedicated studying, My hands-on experience building agentic workloads on AWS was enough to pass.
