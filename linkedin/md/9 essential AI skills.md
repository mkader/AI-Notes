1. Write better prompts: Ask questions the right way so the AI gives clear answers.

2. Automate tasks: Connect apps and let them handle tasks for you.

3. Build AI agents: Make a bot that breaks tasks into small steps, thinks through each one, and sends back the result.

4. Link AI to your data: Let the AI search your PDFs, docs, or database and quote the answer it finds.

5. Use Multimodal AI: Send text, pictures, or code in one message and get a single, combined reply.

6. Create Your Own AI Helper: Adjust a model’s tone, memory, and rules so it fits your project or team.

7. Make Voices & Avatars: Turn words into lifelike speech or a talking video in minutes.

8. Combine Productivity Apps: Mix notes, tasks, and automations into one smooth flow.

9. Create images or videos: Make pictures or short clips from text prompts.
