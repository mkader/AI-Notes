“The best tool is the one you actually use.” — Elon Musk

There are hundreds of AI tools out there.

But here's what separates professionals from hobbyists:

→ They know which tools are best for which jobs.

Here’s a simplified cheat sheet:

🎨 Need visuals?
→ Use Stockimg.ai for AI stock photos
→ Or Clipdrop to clean up existing ones

🌐 Need a site?
→ Durable AI builds it from just a sentence

🧠 Planning & Strategy?
→ MarketPlan.io turns thoughts into strategy

📹 Want to go viral?
→ RunwayML for video, Tome for storytelling

🧰 Bonus tools:
→ Iloveimg.com for image editing
→ Microsoft Designer for Canva-style visuals powered by AI
→ Predis.ai for full social content (with ChatGPT built-in)
