* Gartner outlines three types of roles shaping the future of AI-

* Core AI Roles - Roles that are foundational for AI operations from data to deployment
  1. Model Manager - Manages the full lifecycle of AI/ML models, including development, deployment, and reuse.
  2. Analytics Engineer - Prepares, transforms, and optimizes data for analytics and machine learning consumption.
  3. AI Architect - Designs end-to-end AI systems and ensures they align with organizational architecture.
  4. AI Risk and Governance Specialist - Oversees AI compliance, ethics, and risk management across data and model usage.
  5. AI Product Manager - Defines strategy, vision, and execution plans for AI-powered products and solutions.
  6. UX Designer - Crafts user-centric experiences that make AI interactions intuitive and human-aligned.
  7. AI Developer - Implements AI features and integrates models into products and business applications.

* Must-Have AI Roles - Critical roles for scaling AI responsibly and effectively across the enterprise
  1. ML Engineer - Designs, builds, and productionizes machine learning models for real-world deployment.
  2. Data Engineer - Builds and maintains reliable data infrastructure to enable large-scale AI workflows.
  3. Prompt Engineer - Writes and refines prompts to guide large language models toward accurate outputs.
  4. Head of AI - Leads the organization’s AI direction, strategy, and cross-functional alignment.
  5. D&A and AI Translator - Connects data teams with business stakeholders by translating technical insights into value.
  6. Data Scientist - Develops models and conducts deep data analysis to drive predictive and prescriptive insights.
  7. AI Ethicist - Assesses AI for fairness, transparency, and potential societal impact.

* Emerging AI Roles - Newer roles rising quickly to address the evolving complexity of AI
  1. Model Validator - Validates AI models to ensure they are reliable, unbiased, and regulatory-compliant.
  2. Knowledge Engineer - Builds structured knowledge bases and ontologies to enhance AI reasoning.
  3. Decision Engineer - Designs decision automation systems that combine logic, data, and AI models.
