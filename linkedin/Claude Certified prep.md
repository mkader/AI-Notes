Recently cleared both the Claude Certified Associate and Claude Certified Architect-Professional exams. Sharing how I prepped, in case it helps anyone else planning to take these.

📘 For the Associate exam:

1️⃣ Start with the official prep course on the Anthropic Partner Portal. It's a quick skim, a couple of hours is enough. 
2️⃣ Take couple of mock tests 
3️⃣ If you've already sat through AI fluency trainings from various channels, a lot of this will feel familiar. You could probably wing it, but the prep course is still worth it just to get comfortable with Anthropic's specific terminology or claudish as people call it now :)

📗 For the Architect Professional exam:

1️⃣ The official Anthropic prep course again covers everything the exam actually tests. Can be read in 1-2 days.
2️⃣ Found the practice sets on these links worth doing: 
1) https://lnkd.in/gjZVm4yv
2) https://lnkd.in/gP7cEiP6
3️⃣ For revision, this free video has a great 1-hour quick reference video that summarizes the content well: 🔗 https://lnkd.in/gpH-JxxU 
4️⃣ Tip: don't over-index on trivia. Focus on patterns : which approach and integration fits which scenario , which optimization method best suits a task , where a guardrail should actually live in the architecture, recognizing failure modes , how to think about compliance in sensitive domains etc. That's what the exam is really testing.
