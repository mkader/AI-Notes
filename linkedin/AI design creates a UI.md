AI design creates a services layer beyond UI.

I had a great Glaringly Obvious chat with Sean J. Savage. He explains how AI creates a new “services” layer beyond UI and business strategy, shifting how products grow and change. Instead of building stable systems, we now face shifting behaviors, improvisations, and emergent patterns. The real challenge is figuring out how to keep pace and how to know what’s truly working. Check out our conversation: https://lnkd.in/gSpPy7Wu

We cover a number of topics:

→ Why AI isn’t deterministic: same input, different output.
→ The new services layer: LLM shifts reshape design.
→ When AI improvises: systems improvise, quality risks.
→ The feedback loop problem: AI feedback remains unsolved.

Our conversation reflects my own experiences while designing for AI. The biggest lesson? Don’t assume anything. Even experienced teams can run into unexpected issues. I capture it in the diagram, and also write about it: https://lnkd.in/gmM7WR-W
