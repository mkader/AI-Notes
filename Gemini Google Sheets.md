NEWS: Gemini now works in Google Sheets. 

Here’s how to use it: 

1. Open Google Sheets.
2. Search for 'Ask Gemini' icon at the top right.
3. Click it to open the Gemini side pane.
4. Select a suggested prompt or write your own.

You can: 
1. Generate formulas.
2. Write instant emails. 
3. Summarize feedback. 
4. Automate categories. 
5. Build charts and graphs.
